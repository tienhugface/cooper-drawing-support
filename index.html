Cooper Drawing Support

Welcome to Cooper Drawing! This app helps children learn handwriting, shapes, numbers, and drawing through guided tracing.

📌 How to Use the App

  • Select a pencil color from the palette
  • Choose a tracing mode (letters, numbers, shapes, or free drawing)
  • Follow the grey guide and trace on the canvas
  • Use Clear to reset the canvas

📷 Camera Not Working?

  • Go to iPad/iPhone Settings
  • Find Cooper Drawing
  • Enable Camera permission

🧒 Common Issues

  • Drawing not appearing → restart app
  • Shape not generating → reselect shape mode
  • Lag → close background apps

🔒 Privacy

Cooper Drawing does not collect, store, or upload any personal data. All drawings and photos remain on the device.
