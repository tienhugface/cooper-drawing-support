Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Cooper Drawing is designed to help children practice drawing and tracing.

Information Collection

Cooper Drawing does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Photos and Camera

The app may access the device camera and photo library only when the user chooses to create tracing patterns from photos. Photos remain on the user's device and are not uploaded to any server.

Data Storage

Drawings and app data are stored locally on the device.

Third-Party Services

Cooper Drawing does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support page.
